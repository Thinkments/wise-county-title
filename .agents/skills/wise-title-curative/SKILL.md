# Wise Title Curative & TDI Rate Engine Skill

## 1. Promulgated TDI Rate Calculations

All title insurance premiums in Texas are strictly promulgated by the Texas Department of Insurance (TDI) under Texas Insurance Code Chapter 2703:

- **Basic Manual Rule R-1:** Standard rates calculated per $1,000 tier brackets.
- **Rule R-5 Simultaneous Issue Discount:** When Owner's Policy and Lender's Policy are issued simultaneously in the same transaction, the Lender's Policy is discounted to a flat **$100**.
- **Statutory Endorsements:**
  - T-19 / T-19.1 (Minerals & Restrictions): 5% of Basic Rate (Residential) / 10% (Commercial).
  - T-38 (Area & Boundary Coverage): 5% of Basic Rate.
  - T-42 / T-42.1 (Texas Home Equity Loan Endorsements).
  - T-26 (Additional Insured Endorsement): $25.

## 2. Texas Curative Title Rules & Statutes

- **4-Year Mortgage Statute of Limitations (CPRC §16.035):** If a deed of trust states a specific maturity date, any lien is extinguished as a matter of law 4 years after that date without requiring formal court action.
- **Affidavits of Heirship (Texas Estates Code §203):** Used for non-probated estates where deceased passed over 4 years prior without outstanding debts. Requires 2 disinterested affiants.
- **Lady Bird Deeds (Enhanced Life Estate):** Grantor retains lifetime right to sell, lease, or mortgage without remainderman joinder.
- **T-47 Real Property Affidavit:** Accompanying existing surveys to certify whether boundaries, fences, or improvements have changed.
