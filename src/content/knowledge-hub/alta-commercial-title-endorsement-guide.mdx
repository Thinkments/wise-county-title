## Commercial Title Endorsements in Texas

Unlike many states where title underwriters have wide discretion to draft bespoke endorsements, commercial title insurance in Texas is strictly regulated by the **Texas Department of Insurance (TDI)**. All endorsements must utilize promulgated **T-Series forms** with mandatory statutory pricing.

For commercial developers, institutional lenders, and real estate attorneys, choosing the correct endorsement package ensures multi-million dollar assets are protected against zoning challenges, severed mineral disruptions, survey boundary overlaps, and access disputes.

## Deep Dive: The T-19.1 Minerals & Surface Endorsement

In North Texas and the Barnett Shale region, severed mineral estates are ubiquitous. Under Texas law, the mineral estate is the **dominant estate**, granting oil and gas operators the right to use the surface for exploration.

The **Texas Form T-19.1 Endorsement** protects commercial property owners against forced removal of improvements or structural damage caused by future mineral development. Underwriting requirements include:
1. Reviewing existing mineral leases and pooling agreements.
2. Confirming whether historical wellheads are active or plugged.
3. Reviewing municipal surface development waivers or deed restrictions prohibiting surface drilling.
