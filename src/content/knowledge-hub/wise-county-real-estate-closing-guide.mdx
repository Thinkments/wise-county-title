## Welcome to Wise County, Texas

Located directly northwest of the Dallas–Fort Worth Metroplex along the US Highway 81/287 and US Highway 380 corridors, **Wise County** represents one of the most dynamic real estate markets in North Texas. From historic Victorian homes in Decatur and lakefront properties on Lake Bridgeport to working cattle ranches, equine estates, and master-planned residential communities, Wise County attracts homeowners, agricultural operators, and commercial developers alike.

As the oldest title company in the county, **Wise County Title Company** has safeguarded property rights here since 1878.

## 4 Wise County Real Estate Quirks Every Buyer & Agent Must Know

### 1. The Barnett Shale & Severed Mineral Rights
Much of Wise County sits directly atop the prolific Barnett Shale natural gas formation. In most rural and suburban parcels, mineral rights were severed by prior landowners in the 1950s–1980s. Title insurance policies in Wise County exclude mineral rights under Schedule B, making surface use agreements and Texas Form T-19.1 mineral endorsements critical for property buyers.

### 2. Maintaining Agricultural 1-d-1 Productivity Valuations
Wise County features thousands of acres under agricultural tax valuation. Buyers purchasing acreage in Wise County must submit a new **Form 50-129 application** to Wise CAD by April 30th following closing. Wise CAD requires meeting local degree of intensity standards (such as 1 animal unit per 10–15 acres of native pasture).

### 3. On-Site Sewage Facilities (OSSF / Septic) and Water Wells
Outside Decatur and Bridgeport city limits, properties rely on private water wells and OSSF septic systems. Title surveys must verify proper setbacks from property boundaries and wellhead separation distances.
