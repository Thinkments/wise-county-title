## What is a Texas Lady Bird Deed?

In Texas estate planning, avoiding probate while maintaining total property control is a primary goal for property owners. A **Lady Bird Deed** (formally known as an *Enhanced Life Estate Deed*) accomplishes this with maximum legal efficiency.

Unlike a standard life estate deed (which requires remainder beneficiaries to sign off on any future sale or mortgage), an *Enhanced* Life Estate Deed gives the original owner (the grantor) the absolute, unilateral legal power to:
- Sell the property at any time and keep 100% of the proceeds.
- Refinance or take out a Texas Home Equity Loan (HELOC).
- Cancel or change the designated beneficiaries by recording a new deed.

## Lady Bird Deed vs. Transfer on Death Deed (TODD) vs. Traditional Probate

| Feature | Lady Bird Deed (Enhanced Life Estate) | Transfer on Death Deed (TODD) | Formal Texas Probate (Letters Testamentary) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Avoids Court Probate?** | **Yes (Immediate automatic transfer)** | Yes (Statutory transfer) | No (Requires County Court hearing) |
| **Power to Sell without Beneficiary Joinder?** | **Yes (100% unilateral power)** | Yes (Unilateral power) | N/A |
| **Protects Against Medicaid MERP?** | **Yes (Key Medicaid planning tool)** | Yes | No (Probate estate subject to MERP) |
| **Allows Power of Attorney (POA) Signing?** | **Yes (Permitted with statutory POA)** | **No** (Texas statute forbids POA on TODD) | Yes |
| **Warranty of Title Covenants?** | Can convey with General/Special Warranty | No warranties of title | Executor Deed without warranty |
| **Title Underwriting Acceptance** | **Universally accepted by Texas underwriters** | Strict 2-year creditor waiting periods | Court order required |

## How Title Passes When the Grantor Dies

When the owner of a property under a recorded Lady Bird Deed passes away:
1. **Title Transfers Automatically:** Ownership vests immediately in the remainder beneficiaries named in the deed as a matter of law.
2. **Curative Title Clearance:** To establish marketable title for future sales or refinancing, the beneficiaries simply record a **Certified Copy of the Death Certificate** with the Wise County Clerk.
3. **Zero Probate Filings Required:** No attorneys need to file in probate court, no letters testamentary are required, and no creditor notice periods delay ownership.
