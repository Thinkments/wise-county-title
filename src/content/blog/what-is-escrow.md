## Understanding Escrow

When buying or selling real estate, substantial sums of money and legal property titles change hands. To ensure neither party is put at risk, Texas transactions utilize an impartial third party known as an **escrow agent** or **title company**.

At **Wise County Title Company**, serving Wise County since 1878, our escrow officers hold earnest money deposits, mortgage proceeds, and executed warranty deeds in a secure trust account until all contractual obligations under the TREC contract are satisfied.

### Key Roles of Escrow:
- **Holding Earnest Money:** Depositing and holding funds safely under Texas Department of Insurance regulations.
- **Title Clearance:** Ensuring any prior mortgages, liens, or property taxes are paid in full at closing.
- **Impartial Disbursement:** Distributing sales proceeds to the seller only after the buyer's lender approves funding and the deed is signed.
