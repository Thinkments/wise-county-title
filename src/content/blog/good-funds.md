## What are "Good Funds"?

In Texas, the Department of Insurance enforces strict "Good Funds" regulations (Texas Insurance Code §2651.202). Title companies are legally prohibited from disbursing funds or releasing keys until immediately available, guaranteed funds have been received in escrow.

### Acceptable Forms of Good Funds:
- **Wire Transfers:** The gold standard for instant, guaranteed disbursement.
- **Certified Cashier's Checks:** Accepted for amounts under statutory limits (must be drawn on a Texas financial institution).
- **Personal Checks:** NOT accepted for closing funds due to multi-day bank clearance holds.
