## What is Real Estate Deed Fraud?

Deed fraud occurs when a criminal records a forged deed or fraudulent power of attorney, claiming ownership of a property without the true owner's knowledge, and attempts to sell or borrow against the equity.

### How Title Insurance Protects You:
