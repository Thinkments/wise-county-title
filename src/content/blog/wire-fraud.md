## Real Estate Wire Fraud: A Critical Warning

Real estate transactions are prime targets for sophisticated cyber fraud. Hackers compromise real estate agent or consumer email accounts, monitoring email threads to send fraudulent, last-minute changes to wiring instructions.

### The Golden Rule of Wire Safety:
**Wise County Title Company will NEVER send you changed wiring instructions via email.**
