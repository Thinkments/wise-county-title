## Texas Home Equity Loan (HELOC) Safeguards

Texas has some of the strongest constitutional protections for homeowners in the nation under **Article XVI, Section 50(a)(6) of the Texas Constitution**.

### Strict Texas Home Equity Rules:
- Maximum 80% Loan-to-Value (LTV) combined mortgage debt.
- Mandatory 12-day consumer disclosure notice prior to closing.
- Closings must occur at the permanent office of a title company or attorney.
