## What Happens on Closing Day?

Closing day is the culmination of your real estate transaction. You will meet with your dedicated escrow officer in our private, comfortable closing suites in Decatur or Bridgeport to review and sign your legal documents.

### What to Bring:
- Unexpired government-issued photo ID (Driver's License, US Passport).
- Confirmed bank wire receipt for required closing funds.
- Any lender-mandated stipulations.
