## What is a Real Estate Lien?

A lien is a legal claim or security interest placed upon a piece of real estate to secure the payment of a debt or legal obligation. If a property is sold with unreleased liens, those debts can follow the property, clouding title for the new buyer.

### Common Types of Liens Uncovered in Title Searches:
- **Mortgage Deeds of Trust:** Outstanding home loans or HELOCs.
- **Mechanic's & Materialman's Liens:** Contractors or subcontractors unpaid for home improvements.
- **Property & IRS Tax Liens:** Delinquent county, school, or federal taxes.
- **Abstract of Judgments:** Court money judgments against the seller.
