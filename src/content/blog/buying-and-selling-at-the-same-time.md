## Managing Simultaneous Real Estate Transactions

Coordinating the sale of your existing home with the purchase of your next home requires synchronization between lenders, escrow officers, and movers.

Using Texas Temporary Seller Leasebacks (TREC Form 15-5) and coordinating simultaneous funding with Wise County Title allows homeowners to transition equity seamlessly from their sale into their new purchase.
