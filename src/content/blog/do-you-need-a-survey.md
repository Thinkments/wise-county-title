## The Role of a Land Title Survey

A land title survey provides a precise visual and legal map of a property's boundary lines, showing easements, building setback lines, fence locations, and physical improvements.

Without a current survey or a compliant T-47 affidavit with Area and Boundary coverage, standard title policies exclude any boundary shortages, fence discrepancies, or encroachments.
