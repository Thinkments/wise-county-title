## Why Do Mortgage Lenders Require Title Insurance?

Before a mortgage lender will disburse loan proceeds on real estate, they require guarantee that their Deed of Trust holds a first-priority, unclouded lien position.

Title companies provide lenders with Closing Protection Letters (CPL), Title Commitments, and Lender's Title Policies (Form T-2) to ensure full compliance with TRID and institutional secondary market standards.
