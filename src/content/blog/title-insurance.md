## What Makes Title Insurance Different?

Unlike health, auto, or home insurance (which protect against future potential accidents or disasters for an ongoing monthly premium), **title insurance protects against past defects** that occurred in the property's chain of title before you bought it.

### Best of All:
You pay a **one-time title insurance premium at closing**, and the policy protects your equity and legal ownership for as long as you or your heirs own the property.
