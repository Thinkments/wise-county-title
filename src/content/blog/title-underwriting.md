## Behind the Scenes: Title Underwriting

Before a closing takes place, experienced title examiners review decades of deed records, probate proceedings, easements, and tax assessments.

At Wise County Title Company, our team utilizes the only private sovereignty title plant in Wise County, examining original tract books and digital indexes to ensure that every property we insure transfers free and clear of unreleased encumbrances.
