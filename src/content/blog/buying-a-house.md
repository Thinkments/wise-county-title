## Buying a House in Texas

Buying a home is one of the most exciting financial milestones in life. To ensure your purchase proceeds smoothly from contract execution to closing day, following a structured roadmap prevents surprises.

### Homebuyer Checklist:
1. Deliver earnest money and option fees to Wise County Title within 3 days.
2. Complete home inspections and negotiate repair amendments during the option period.
3. Review your Texas Title Commitment (Schedule B and Schedule C).
4. Review your Loan Estimate and 3-day Closing Disclosure (CD).
5. Bring valid government-issued photo ID and wire closing funds securely.
