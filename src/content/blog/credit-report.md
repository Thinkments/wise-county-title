## How Credit Judgments Impact Title

When an individual loses a court lawsuit or fails to pay a debt, the creditor can record an **Abstract of Judgment** in the county property records. In Texas, this creates a cloud on title that attaches to real property.

Title companies research these public records to determine whether a judgment attaches to a homestead or must be satisfied at closing.
