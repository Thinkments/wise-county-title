## What to Look For When Buying Texas Land

Purchasing rural acreage in Wise County, Montague County, or Parker County is very different from buying a residential home in a suburban subdivision. From private water wells and septic clearances to severed mineral rights and agricultural tax valuations, due diligence is essential.

### 4 Pillars of Rural Land Due Diligence:
1. **1-d-1 Agricultural Exemption Status:** Check whether the land currently holds an active ag valuation with the Wise County Appraisal District (Wise CAD).
2. **Access & Easements:** Verify whether the property has dedicated direct access to a county road or if it relies on a private ingress/egress easement.
3. **Severed Minerals & Accommodation Doctrine:** Confirm if mineral rights have been severed and whether active oil/gas wellheads exist on the tract.
4. **Boundary Surveys:** Metes and bounds descriptions require an accurate survey to delineate fence lines and high-water marks.
