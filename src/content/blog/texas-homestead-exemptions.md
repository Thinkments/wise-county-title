## Lowering Your Property Taxes in Wise County

Filing a homestead exemption on your primary residence is one of the easiest and most impactful ways to lower your property tax bill in Texas.

### Key Benefits:
- **School District Exemption:** At least $100,000 off your home's taxable value for school taxes.
- **10% Appraisal Cap:** Limits annual increases in your home's assessed value.
- **Over 65 & Disabled Tax Ceilings:** Permanently freezes school district taxes.
